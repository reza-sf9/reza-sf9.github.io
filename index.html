Research

My Ph.D. work at WPI's CareLab, advised by Prof. Emmanuel Agu, turns wound photographs and clinical notes into decisions a nurse or clinician can check. Before that I modeled neural signals and built deep learning systems for organ transplantation.

Reading wounds from images

Classification and assessment models for pressure ulcers, diabetic foot ulcers and wound infection, built for clinical decision support. Three U.S. patents came out of this line of work.

Models that give reasons

Multimodal LLM pipelines that combine prompt design, fine-tuning, RAG and visual RAG, and agentic reflection, so a severity grade arrives with a rationale a clinician can verify.

Systems that run

Reproducible workflows for preprocessing, label handling, evaluation and error analysis, with GPU training and inference on Linux, Docker, AWS and Azure. I started as a backend engineer and still write production-minded code.

Publications

A few papers I'm most excited about. The full list is on Google Scholar.

Medical AI and multimodal models

  1. FT-ARM: Fine-Tuned Agentic Reflection Multimodal Language Model for Pressure Ulcer Severity Classification with Reasoning

    A fine-tuned LLaMA 3.2 90B that checks its own pressure ulcer staging in an agentic reflection loop and explains the result. 85% accuracy on Stages I–IV, 4 points above earlier CNN models.

    arXiv preprint, 2025

  2. Multimodal AI for Home Wound Patient Referral Decisions from Images with Specialist Annotations

    Combines smartphone wound photos with clinical notes to help visiting nurses decide when a patient should see a wound specialist.

    IEEE Journal of Translational Engineering in Health and Medicine, 2025

  3. Infection-Reasoner: A Compact Vision-Language Model for Wound Infection Classification with Evidence-Grounded Clinical Reasoning

    A 4B-parameter vision-language model distilled from GPT-5.1 reasoning and refined with reinforcement learning. It beats GPT-5.1 at spotting wound infection (86.8% accuracy), and wound experts judged most of its rationales correct.

    arXiv preprint, 2026

  4. Agentic Medical Knowledge Graphs Enhance Medical Question Answering

    An agent that builds and keeps updating a medical knowledge graph, then reasons over it to answer clinical questions, outperforming some models 10 to 100 times its size.

    Findings of EMNLP, 2025
